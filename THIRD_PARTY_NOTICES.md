# Attribution and third-party notices

The voltage-source and float32-faithful hysteresis implementation derives from
Yuanhang Zhang's original repository, preserved under
`archive/references/yuanhangzhang98-collective_dynamics_neuristor-217d4f0/`.
The ideal-current extension and workflow library are maintained in this project.

Primary model references:

- Zhang et al. (2024), *Collective dynamics and long-range order in thermal
  neuristor networks*, Nature Communications 15, 6986. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-51254-4.
- de Almeida et al. (2002), *Modeling of the hysteretic metal-insulator
  transition in a vanadium dioxide infrared detector*, Optical Engineering
  41(10), 2582–2588. DOI: 10.1117/1.1501095.
- Experimental context: Gildor et al. (2026), *Harnessing the VO2 Phase Transition
  for Automatic Gain Control in Transimpedance Amplifiers*, arXiv:2604.04594v1.
