\section{Current-source formulation}
\label{sec:current_source}

Our objective is to reproduce and predict the current-driven \VOtwo{} response
measured by Gildor \emph{et al.} in their transimpedance-amplifier experiments
\cite{gildor2026}. We therefore replace Zhang's voltage source and load
resistor with an ideal source that imposes the experimental current waveform
$I_{\mathrm{in}}(t)$. The \VOtwo{} element itself is unchanged: its capacitance
and hysteretic resistance remain connected in parallel.

\begin{figure}[H]

              node[circ]{} node[above left]{\(V\)} coordinate (Vnode);

        \node[left] at (-0.15,-0.8) {\(I_{\mathrm{in}}(t)\)};

        \coordinate (LeftTop) at (2.4,0.8);
        \coordinate (LeftBot) at (2.4,-0.8);

    \label{fig:current_source_circuit}

At node $V$, the imposed current divides between the capacitive and resistive
branches. Kirchhoff's current law therefore gives

\begin{equation}
    I_{\mathrm{in}}(t)
    =C\frac{\dd V}{\dd t}+\frac{V}{R(T,\mathcal H)},
    \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
    C\frac{\dd V}{\dd t}
    =I_{\mathrm{in}}(t)-\frac{V}{R(T,\mathcal H)}.
    \label{eq:current_source_balance}
\end{equation}

An external series resistor may change the voltage required from the source,
but it does not change the imposed current and therefore does not enter
Eq.~\eqref{eq:current_source_balance}. The thermal balance and hysteretic
resistance law remain unchanged.
