# Supplementary figures and animations

The files here make the current visual evidence easy to browse without moving it away
from its source.

## Figures

- `yuanhang_current_validation.png`: validation of nonzero current-driven voltage
  oscillations using the Yuanhang resistance model.
- `resistance_temperature_trajectory.png`: simulated R(T) trajectory over the Yuanhang
  hysteresis background.
- `sample_resistance_fit.png`: measured and fitted sample R(T) branches with residuals.
- `measured_current_sweep.png`: normalized experimental sweep and measured oscillation
  window.
- `measured_300mV_oscillation_onset.png`: baseline-corrected current and voltage for
  the first coherently oscillating experimental record.
- `measured_250mV_pre_onset.png`: matching non-oscillating record immediately below
  onset.
- `measured_oscillation_onset_bracket.png`: direct 250 versus 300 mV comparison on
  shared axes and under the same detection window.
- `environmental_conductance.png`: settled pre-onset trace used for `S_e`.
- `thermal_capacitance.png`: heating-edge fits used for `C_th` and `tau_th`.
- `model_vs_experiment.png`: all-current blind comparison of measured and predicted
  voltage extrema, mean, amplitude, and frequency.
- `model_vs_experiment_traces.png`: representative waveform overlays below onset and
  across the measured oscillation window.
- `specimen_capacitance_sensitivity.png`: frequency maps over electrical and thermal
  capacitance, including the adopted point and out-of-bound stress tests.

## Animations

- `current_voltage_rt_evolution.gif`: report animation pairing current/voltage versus
  time with the R(T) trajectory.
- Files beginning with `historical_` come from the July 2026 presentation package.
  They remain useful presentation material but predate the current sample-parameter
  identification and are not quantitative evidence for the final fitted sample.

`historical_presentation_package/` exposes the complete earlier package, including its
figures, animations, source, and notes, without duplicating it here.
